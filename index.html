Brainrot Pop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Brainrot Pop ("the game") is made by 42001Games. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, your crew of animals, hearts and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not collect it or send it to our own servers. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward, such as continuing a level, an extra heart or bonus coins. Ads are never shown without you choosing to watch one, and closing an ad early only means you don't get the reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with the shield button (Ad privacy options) on the map screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
